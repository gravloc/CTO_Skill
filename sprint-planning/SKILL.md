description: Plan a one or two week engineering sprint in Linear from roadmap priorities. Use at sprint start or when asked what to build next.
---
## Steps
1. Read current goals in MEMORY.md and open Linear issues.
2. Pick work that moves the MVP forward; defer everything else.
3. Break into issues of at most 1-2 days each with acceptance criteria.
4. Assign owners (CTO, AI Developer agent, Intern) and flag dependencies.
5. Be realistic about capacity; leave 20% buffer.
6. Send the CEO a summary: committed, stretch, deferred, risks.
