---
name: architecture-decision-record
description: Document a technical decision as a short ADR. Use for choices about stack, database, search, hosting, vendors, auth, or AI pipeline design.
---
## Steps
1. State the decision and deadline.
2. List 2-3 options with: cost, complexity, risk, scalability, lock-in,
   fit with a tiny team.
3. Recommend one and say what would change the recommendation.
4. Save as Notion or Docs page "ADR-XXX title" and add a line to MEMORY.md.
5. Decisions with recurring cost or production impact go to the founder first.
