---
name: tech-status-report
description: Weekly technical status report for the CEO. Use every Friday or when asked for engineering status.
---
## Steps
1. GitHub: merged PRs, open PRs, failing CI. Linear: done, slipped, blocked.
2. Summarize: Shipped, In progress, Blocked, Risks (security, scope, debt),
   Decisions needed, Next week.
3. Mark each roadmap item on track / at risk / off track.
4. Keep to one page, post to Slack for the CEO and save to Drive.
